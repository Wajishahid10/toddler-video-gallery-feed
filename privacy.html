Toddler Video Library — Privacy policy

Summary

Toddler Video Library is two Android apps: a parent app that sets the rules and a kid app that plays only what those rules allow. Your family's data is kept in your own Google Drive and on your own devices. The developer does not run a server that receives it, and the apps contain no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

What is stored, and where

In your Google Drive: the rules you set (trusted and banned channels, topics, categories, hidden or featured videos, clips), your settings (daily limit, languages, banned words, a salted hash of the parent PIN, your child's first name if you enter it), the child's watch history (which video, when, for how long; kept 90 days), "hide this" requests from the kid tablet, and any exports you make.

On your devices: copies of those files for offline use, recently fetched video details, and clips you allowed for offline viewing.

The child does not create an account and is not asked for any personal information.

Your Google account

You sign in with Google so the apps can read and write their own files in your Drive. They ask only for "drive.file" (files this app created) and, in the hidden storage mode, "drive.appdata" (the app's private folder). They cannot see your other Drive files. Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide these features and is not transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by people; this follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The apps use YouTube API Services to find videos from channels you trust and to check that each video is public, embeddable and not age-restricted. By using the apps you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) describes how Google handles data.

Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may still collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy when a video plays.

Video details from the YouTube API (IDs, titles, thumbnails, durations) are stored on your devices and refreshed or deleted within 30 days. You can revoke the apps' access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Shared video list, Pexels and the Internet Archive

A list of suggested channels and new videos is published daily on GitHub Pages; the apps download it but send nothing to it. Clips you add from Pexels or the Internet Archive are streamed or downloaded from their servers, which see the usual connection details (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Parent app: Settings → Google account → "Delete my data" deletes the app's files and exports from your Drive and this phone, and removes the app's access to your Google account.

Kid tablet: parent area → Google account → "Remove this tablet's data", or uninstall the app.

Without the app: in Google Drive go to Settings → Manage apps → Toddler Video Library → "Delete hidden app data", delete the "Toddler Video Library" folders from My Drive, and remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
